04 — A note from our ISO

"Service, like a home, is built with warmth, patience and care."

I'm Arathi Swamy, International Service Organiser of Inner Wheel Club of Bangalore Banashankari, District 319 — writing on behalf of our President, Mrs. Kusuma Raghavendra, and every member of our club.

I'm a homemaker who completed my PUC, married to Mr. Haluswamy — an officer with KSRTC — for 29 wonderful years. Between the newspaper, some music and a good book, I find my quiet moments in cooking. I joined Inner Wheel in 2019, and serving as ISO for 2026–27 is an honour I don't take lightly.

To me, ISO isn't just a title — it's a chance to reach past our own club, build real friendships, and bring communities a little closer together. Here's to a year full of warmth, shared moments and new friendships.

Arathi SwamyISO — Inner Wheel Club of Bangalore Banashankari
